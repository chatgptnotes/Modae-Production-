02 Opportunity Management Workflow

The headline problem. The diagram is organised around one primary split — Greenfield (Project, Upgrade) versus Brownfield (Retrofit, Service, Spares) — and almost every downstream rule depends on which side an opportunity falls. That field does not exist in the application. Searching the source for “brownfield” returns zero results. The earlier clickable prototype this app was ported from did carry a Greenfield/Brownfield context on every opportunity; it was dropped during the rewrite and never replaced. Everything in Sections 2, 3 and 7 of the diagram inherits this gap.

Section 5 — Management Approval

This is the second structural divergence. The diagram routes approvals on order value × margin. The application routes them on gross-margin percentage × discount percentage. These are different variables, different thresholds and different approvers — the implemented rule cannot be reconciled with the drawn one by tuning settings.
DiagramWhat we actually builtVerdict
5A. Technical Approval — approver LJS or AN A technical deviation approval exists, but it is decided by LJS only. The role “AN” does not exist anywhere in the application — it is not in the role list, the permissions matrix or any seed data. The technical reviewer persona that does exist has read-only rights and cannot approve anything. Different A named approver is missing entirely.
5B. Commercial Approval — approver AH only Commercial deviation and Amber credit terms both route to AH. Matches
5C. Margin Approval matrix
< 10 Lakh & > 50% → Assigned Salesperson
< 10 Lakh & ≤ 50% → AH or LJS
> 10 Lakh & > 50% → AH + LJS
> 10 Lakh & ≤ 50% → AH + LJS		 Not implemented. The implemented rule is: margin at or above 25% and discount at or below 5% → LJS alone; margin at or above 20% and discount at or below 10% → LJS alone; otherwise LJS + AH. Order value is calculated and displayed but never used to route an approval, the ₹10 Lakh breakpoint appears nowhere, the 50% margin breakpoint appears nowhere, and there is no tier that lets the assigned salesperson self-approve. Recommended order of work

Tier 1 — Data-model corrections
Small, cheap changes that unblock everything downstream. Restore the Greenfield / Brownfield field on the opportunity and split the routing so Retrofit stops inheriting the Project workbench (O1). Rewrite the commercial gate as the diagram’s order-value × margin matrix and add the missing AN approver role (O5, O6). Add a region field to the lead and make the ownership rules an executed lookup rather than a prompt hint, with the override restricted to LJS/AH and a reason recorded (L3). Add the seven-value lead-source list (L1). Fix the handover acceptance-key defect.
Tier 2 — Missing workflow objects
B-01…B-05 as tracked steps with assigned-salesperson sign-off, reusing the approval record already in place (O3). The service site-survey sub-flow feeding the existing rate-sheet calculator (O4). Mandatory re-approval on post-release revision, with typed revisions routing back to the matching B-step (O7, O8). Enforce the loss reason on close and add competitor fields (O11, O12). Enforce the Greenfield no-pricing boundary (O2).
Tier 3 — Infrastructure — the honest blockers
A scheduler, so reminders, the one-week KYC and fee expiry, validity warnings and approval SLA nudges actually fire (L5, O10). A server-side send path that attaches the PDF, plus a Teams webhook — together these unlock the Teams chatbot validation, the AI-08 notification and the multi-channel dispatch lane (L4, O9). A discarded-lead archive and AI-action audit logging (L6, L10). These items depend on client decisions about hosting and the Microsoft tenancy and should be raised rather than built on assumption.
